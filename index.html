Why Four4

Built around reps, not lectures

Every session follows a proven 60-minute structure — warm-up, technical drill, skill game, small-sided play, and a cool-down recap — so kids leave every day having actually played, not just practiced.

1

Small Groups, Real Attention

24 campers, 3 coaches — a 1:8 ratio means every camper gets coached, not just supervised.

2

No Cookie-Cutter Drills

Week 1 is an evaluation. Week 2 is built around what each camper actually needs — not the same script for every player.

3

Skill Days, Not Just Scrimmage

Dedicated days for dribbling, passing, shooting, and speed & agility — plus fun games and a mini match day to apply it all.

The Four4 Difference

Not a cookie-cutter camp

Running the same twelve drills for every kid isn't coaching — it's crowd control. Four4 splits the two weeks into an evaluation phase and a personalized phase instead, so week two actually fits the camper in front of us.

Week 1 — Evaluate

We watch before we plan

Every camper works through the same fundamentals — dribbling, passing, first touch, shooting — while coaches track where each kid is strong and where they're still developing.

→
Week 2 — Personalize

Then we coach to it

Sessions shift toward what each camper specifically needs — more 1v1 reps for a kid ready to attack defenders, extra first-touch work for a kid who needs it — inside the same group, not a separate program.
